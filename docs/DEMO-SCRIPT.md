# DAMC — Demo Day Script (5 minutes)

## Setup Before Demo

- Claude Code open on screen, terminal visible to audience
- Browser ready to open HTML report
- Backup: pre-generated report HTML in case of network issues

---

## Script

### [0:00 - 0:30] Hook — The Question

> "Everyone in this room has asked themselves one question in the past year:
> *Will AI replace me?*
>
> But here's the real problem — nobody has a data-driven answer.
> Every 'AI readiness' quiz is just a survey. You tell it what you think about yourself.
>
> What if AI could actually *look at how you use AI* — and tell you the truth?"

### [0:30 - 1:00] Introduce DAMC

> "DAMC is a Claude Code skill that evaluates your value in the AI era.
> Not through surveys. Through scanning your actual AI environment.
>
> Four dimensions:
> - **D** — Distillation Value: Is your expertise worth bottling into an AI?
> - **A** — Anti-Distillation: What can AI never take from you?
> - **M** — AI Mastery: How well do you actually wield AI tools?
> - **C** — Career Compass: Where should you go next?
>
> Let me show you."

### [1:00 - 2:30] Live Demo — Run DAMC

> "I'm going to run DAMC right now, on my own machine."

**[Type in Claude Code]**: `/damc`

**[Privacy notice appears]** — Type "agree"

**[Scan runs with live progress]**:
> "Watch — it's scanning my actual Claude Code environment right now.
> 83 skills... 12 memory files... 8 MCP servers... 45 AI-collaborative git commits.
> This is real data, not a quiz."

**[3 questions]** — Answer quickly

**[Report generates]**

> "30 seconds. From trigger to full career assessment."

### [2:30 - 3:30] Show the Report

**[Open HTML report in browser]**

> "Here's my report. I'm an AI Architect — high expertise, high irreplaceability, high AI fluency.
>
> But look at this — my cross-domain thinking score is only 68. That's my biggest gap.
> And DAMC tells me exactly what to do about it in the next 90 days.
>
> This isn't a personality test. It's a career diagnostic built on real data."

**[Scroll to insights section]**

> "It even identifies which of my skills are worth distilling into AI,
> and which abilities are my moat — the things AI can never replicate."

### [3:30 - 4:15] The Share Moment

> "And because everyone loves sharing their results..."

**[Click 'Copy Share Card' button]**

> "One click — ready for Twitter or LinkedIn.
> This is how DAMC grows: people share their archetype like they share their MBTI type."

### [4:15 - 4:45] Business Model & Vision

> "DAMC has three tiers:
> - **Free**: Full local report via Claude Code skill — open source
> - **Pro**: Platform with historical tracking, deeper analysis
> - **Enterprise**: Team benchmarking — 'How AI-ready is your engineering department?'
>
> The framework is language-agnostic, role-universal, and works for any AI tool ecosystem.
> Today it's Claude Code. Tomorrow it's VS Code, Cursor, Windsurf."

### [4:45 - 5:00] Close

> "In the AI era, knowing your value is the first step to multiplying it.
>
> DAMC. Know your worth.
>
> Thank you."

## 🎤 Optional Power Move: Scan a Judge

If time permits or during Q&A:

> "Would any judge like to try? I can run DAMC on your machine right now — 30 seconds."

If a judge volunteers:
1. They open Claude Code on their laptop
2. Run `/damc`
3. Show their result on the big screen

**This is the most memorable moment possible.** A judge seeing their own assessment, live, creates an emotional connection no slide deck can match.
